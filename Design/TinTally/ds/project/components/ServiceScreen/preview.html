The pattern

Nameplate on two strings ("Building · Service", building glyph). One menu plate, 1728 x 796 at (96, 140): the list (560) of what the service works on, the tray with the selected one hung from the rail, the counter (quote rows, one plain line, the action keycap). The hint bar under it. Instances: Mage Tower · Identify, Alchemist · Restock.

Gamepad (not drawn)

The hint bar reads "B Back | A Restock"; the D-pad and left stick move the list; A restocks the selected row. Mouse: click a row to select it, click the keycap to restock.

Numbers

Real: Health Potion stacks to 10. Example: the shelf sizes, the field caps, the batch sizes, the stock list and the tier gate. The field-cap model, the restock clock and the building tiers are TODO(DECISION).
