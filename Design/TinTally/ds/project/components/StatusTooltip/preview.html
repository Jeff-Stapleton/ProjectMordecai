Where it appears

When a chip in CharacterTab's "Active statuses" row (under its header strip) takes hover or focus. Above is that row with its markup and the example moment (Frostbitten 2 of 4, Focused); the HUD chips never take focus in play.

How it hangs

On the top layer, under the chip, on a 20 px light string from below the chip's hem. It overlays the menu and never moves the layout. Mouse leaves or focus moves: it goes at once.

Gamepad

The D-pad or left stick moves focus between chips and the tooltip follows focus; no extra button opens it.
