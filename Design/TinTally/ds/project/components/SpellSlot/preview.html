How it works (control_bindings_v1.1)

Hold Skill A (Q / RB) or Skill B (E / LB): up to 8 known spells ring the player. Aim with the mouse or the right stick; release to equip the aimed one. A tap still casts the equipped skill. Time does not slow, so there is no veil.

States drawn

Fireball is equipped (Selected, enamel). Blink is aimed (the focus ring). Cone of Cold and Magic Missile are example spells. Two empty sockets are dashed (nothing to pick).

Not built

The game has four direct slots (Q, E, R, 1). A Skill A / B HUD would show two SpellSlots and this radial. Decide with US-097.
