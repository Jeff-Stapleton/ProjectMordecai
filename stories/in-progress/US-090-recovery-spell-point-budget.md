# US-090: Spell point regeneration and respawn refill

**Epic:** Epic R: Playability Recovery
**Priority:** P2
**Depends on:** US-089
**Paired story:** none

## Overview
A fresh character has 10 SP while the arena loadout costs 12 (Fireball 3, Blink 2,
Stone Skin 4, Restoration 3). Add authoritative GAS regeneration at 0.25 SP/s
times `SpellPointsRegenMultiplier`, paused while Dead or Silenced, plus an
immediate full refill on respawn. Features: spells, spell_hud, death_respawn.

## Execution Mode
**HEADLESS**
The native periodic GameplayEffect, authoritative PlayerState application and
respawn restoration are C++. No content asset is required.

## Technical Notes
- Keep the fresh MaxSP default at 10.
- Use one non-stacking infinite native GameplayEffect with a 1 s period and
  `bExecutePeriodicEffectOnApplication=false`.
- Its periodic modifier adds
  `0.25 × SpellPointsRegenMultiplier` to SpellPoints.
- Ongoing tag requirements inhibit the effect while
  `Mordecai.State.Dead` or `Mordecai.Status.Silenced` is present. Cursed keeps
  its existing additive -0.5 regen multiplier.
- Apply the effect exactly once, authority only, in
  `AMordecaiPlayerState::PostInitializeComponents`.
- `AMordecaiCharacter::HandlePlayerRespawn` refills SpellPoints with
  `SetNumericAttributeBase`, beside Health, Stamina and Posture.
- Keep all SP logic in GAS.
- No PIE-only paths: nothing depends on `IsPlayInEditor()`, `WITH_EDITOR`, `LyraDeveloperSettings`, or editor-only state.
